# Research: Generalized open-model inference providers (2026): serving engines, per-token pricing for 7-8B and 70B-class models, prompt/prefix caching, session/KV-retention features, BYOC/dedicated offerings, and latest funding — Fireworks, Together, Baseten, Groq, Cerebras, DeepInfra, Novita, Hyperbolic, Nebius, SambaNova, Lambda, Modal, RunPod, Parasail, and others

## Summary

As of September 2026 the open-model inference providers surveyed split into three groups by stated serving stack. Fireworks (proprietary FireAttention CUDA kernels, FP8/FP4), Together (Together Inference Engine/Turbo with FlashAttention-3/4, Megakernel, ATLAS speculators), and Baseten (Engine-Builder-LLM on TensorRT-LLM; BIS-LLM with KV-aware routing and vLLM-backed example configs; custom vLLM/SGLang allowed) publish engine details. Groq (LPU), Cerebras (WSE), and SambaNova (SN40L RDU) run custom silicon. DeepInfra, Novita, Hyperbolic, Nebius, Parasail, RunPod, Modal and Lambda do not name a production engine on fetched pages; Modal and RunPod document vLLM as a bring-your-own option.

Per-token pricing for Llama 3.1 8B ranges from $0.02/$0.04 (DeepInfra) and $0.02/$0.05 (Novita) to $0.10 flat (Hyperbolic) and Fireworks' 4B-16B tier at $0.20; Groq's 8B is now enterprise-only. Llama 3.3 70B ranges from $0.10/$0.32 (DeepInfra), $0.13/$0.40 (Nebius), $0.135/$0.40 (Novita), $0.22/$0.50 (Parasail), $0.40 (Hyperbolic), $0.60/$1.20 (SambaNova), $0.90 (Fireworks) to $1.04 (Together); Groq's 70B moved to contact-sales; Baseten and Cerebras do not list Llama on their public token APIs; Lambda's Inference API is winding down.

Caching: Fireworks (automatic, ~50% serverless discount, minutes-to-hours retention, session-affinity and isolation headers), Groq (automatic, 50%, 2-hour expiry, GPT-OSS only), Cerebras (automatic, 5-min guaranteed TTL up to 1 hour, no discount), DeepInfra (automatic plus explicit prompt_cache_key and paid 5m/1h retention with cache-write multipliers), Baseten (automatic, cached input at 10%), Parasail/Novita/Together/SambaNova (cache-read prices listed for some models; mechanisms undocumented).

Dedicated/BYOC: Fireworks (GPU-second on-demand, reserved throughput, BYOC private preview incl. on-prem), Baseten (single-tenant, self-hosted in AWS/GCP/OCI, hybrid overflow), Together (per-GPU-minute dedicated endpoints, H100 $3.99/hr), DeepInfra/Nebius/Parasail/Novita (per-GPU-hour dedicated), Cerebras (dedicated endpoints), Groq (GroqMetal bare metal), SambaNova (SambaStack on-prem).

Funding: Fireworks $1.505B Series D at $17.5B (Jul 2026); Together $800M Series C (Jul 2026, valuation undisclosed); Groq $650M (Jun 2026) plus $350M at $3.5B (Aug 2026) after the ~$20B Nvidia license; Cerebras IPO May 2026 (~$95B opening valuation); SambaNova $1B first close at $11B (Jul 2026); DeepInfra $107M Series B (May 2026); Baseten $300M (Jan 2026, secondary); Nebius $5.75B converts (Aug 2026); Lambda $926M term loan (Aug 2026); Modal >$466M total; RunPod $120M ARR.
