# Research: KV-cache infrastructure ecosystem as of 2026-09: LMCache/Tensormesh, Mooncake Store, NVIDIA Dynamo+NIXL+KVBM, llm-d, vLLM production-stack, SGLang HiCache, AIBrix, Kubernetes inference-gateway KV-aware routing, storage-vendor KV offload products, and the state of the vLLM KVConnector V1 / SGLang HiCacheStorage APIs

## Summary

As of September 2026 the KV-cache infrastructure layer consists of several open-source projects plus one venture-funded company built on one of them. LMCache (Apache-2.0, 11.6k stars, 220+ contributors) is a KV-cache layer for vLLM, SGLang and NVIDIA Dynamo with CPU/disk/Redis/Mooncake/S3/NIXL/GDS backends and LRU-family watermark eviction; its creators founded Tensormesh, which raised $4.5M (Oct 2025) and $20M (May 2026, AMD Ventures/CoreWeave/NVentures) and sells Tensormesh Inference, an OpenAI-compatible serverless/reserved SaaS that bills cached input tokens at $0, with an on-prem 'Operator' listed as coming soon and a post-v1 pricing model of 30% of estimated savings. Mooncake (Apache-2.0, 6.5k stars, Moonshot/Kimi's platform) provides a Transfer Engine and a distributed Mooncake Store with approximate-LRU eviction, 10-second leases, soft/hard pins, etcd-based master HA, DRAM plus eager SSD offload (bucket FIFO/LRU), and a not-yet-production DFS tier; a May 2026 vLLM integration reported 3.8x throughput and 46x lower TTFT on 60 GB200s. NVIDIA Dynamo (Apache-2.0, ~8k stars, v1.4.2 Aug 2026) ships KVBM (G1 GPU, G2 host, G3 disk, G4 object; presence/LFU offload filters; now also a standalone kvbm pip package) and NIXL (UCX/GDS/POSIX/S3/Azure/HF3FS/Mooncake plugins), and anchors the CMX/ICMS 'G3.5' flash tier that twelve storage vendors announced for 2H 2026. llm-d (Apache-2.0, CNCF Sandbox Mar 2026; v0.9.0 Aug 17, 2026) provides precise KV-event-driven prefix-cache routing (now default), tiered offloading via vLLM's native OffloadingConnector (LRU/ARC, CPU plus FS/S3/P2P secondary tiers), P2P KV pulling over NIXL, and token-aware routing; its EPP has absorbed the full-featured components from the Kubernetes Gateway API Inference Extension (v1.6.0) and powers GKE Inference Gateway. SGLang HiCache offers L1/L2/L3 with Mooncake, 3FS, NIXL, AIBrix and file backends, private per-instance host memory, and open RFCs for session-aware/agent-aware/programmatic KV hints with TTL-bounded pins. AIBrix (ByteDance) uses S3FIFO/LRU/FIFO L1 eviction and PrisKV/InfiniStore/EIC L2 stores. vLLM's KVConnector V1 remains labeled experimental since its April 2025 merge while hosting in-tree LMCache, NIXL, Mooncake, FlexKV, MoRIIO and offloading connectors. Production trace studies (Alibaba, Claude Code sessions) report ideal hit ratios of 54-62%, P99 KV lifespans of 97 seconds, and inter-turn pauses from 2 seconds to 11 minutes.
